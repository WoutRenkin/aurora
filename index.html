How to watch

  • Get away from street lights. Even 10 minutes out of the centre makes a big difference.
  • Look north first. On strong nights it can fill the whole sky.
  • Give your eyes 15 minutes in the dark, and avoid looking at your phone screen.
  • Faint aurora often looks grey-white to the eye. Your phone camera in night mode will show the green.
  • It comes and goes. Stay out at least an hour if the sky is clear.

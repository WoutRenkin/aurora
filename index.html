    Check with your eyes too: Tromsø Geophysical Observatory magnetometers (UiT) show local activity before any model does, and yr.no has the full local forecast.

    How the verdict works: it needs darkness (sun more than 8° below the horizon), a gap in the clouds, and aurora activity from the OVATION model, with southward Bz as a boost. OVATION gives the chance of aurora overhead; aurora lower on the horizon is often visible when that number is small. Spot coordinates are approximate.
